Case study 01

Xeloka Mobile

A finance & accounting app for recording money in and out, so accountants see transactions as soon as staff enter them.

Live on Google Play & App Store
Role
Built the first version at PT Your Lex Advisor, then rebuilt and released it as the sole mobile developer at PT Hare Business Consulting.
Users
5 client companies, mostly villa management: owners, staff and accountants.
Team
Me on mobile; a separate backend team documents the APIs in Swagger/OpenAPI.

What I did

  • Split the app into 9 Clean Architecture packages with a one-way dependency flow, fixing the circular dependencies of the first version.
  • Migrated push notifications from OneSignal to Firebase Cloud Messaging, including deep linking.
  • Set up 6 GitHub Actions workflows for Android and iOS store releases and over-the-air patches.
  • Added hybrid updates with Shorebird and Remote Config, so hotfixes reach users without waiting for store review.
  • Built offline receipt scanning with on-device ML Kit OCR, keeping financial documents on the phone.
  • Wrote SQLite migrations in single transactions with tests, so existing users never lost data on update.
  • Fixed production crashes found in Crashlytics, including R8 rules and image texture crashes.

Results

  • Live on Google Play and the App Store, used by 5 client companies.
  • Hotfixes reach users through Shorebird patches without waiting for store review.

Case study 02

Xeloka POS

A multiplatform cashier app for clients who sell in stores, built to keep working when the internet drops.

In development · not yet released
Platforms
Android, iOS, Windows, macOS, landscape and keyboard-first.
Payments
Settled through existing bank partnerships, no third-party gateway.

Technical highlights

  • Offline-first sync engine. Sales are saved locally first and uploaded in order when online. Server changes are downloaded as deduplicated, idempotent updates, so nothing is lost or counted twice.
  • Offline tax calculation. Taxes are calculated on the device per product, since some items are taxable and some are not. Cashiers see correct totals without internet, and posted transactions match the backend's calculation.
  • Thermal printing. ESC/POS receipts over Bluetooth, USB and network, plus PDF receipts, tested on real printers.
  • Desktop features. F1–F12 shortcuts, a customer-facing display in a second window, and a Windows installer.
  • Guard tests. Automated checks for architecture rules, design tokens and translation coverage.
